This is a project for learning crowd simulation in Unity. 
The ultimate goal is to create a train station scene where pedestrians board and unboard a train.

Here are more details about the scene:
- There are four stations on the line, and a train continuously moves back and forth between them.
- Each station features a long platform and a single gate.
- Pedestrians spawn at both ends of the long platform. In the center of the platform, a small gate opens to allow pedestrians to board the train.
- Each pedestrian has a specific goal, which is either:
    - 1: Walk to the opposite end of the same platform.
    - 2: Board the train, disembark at another station, and then head to one of the ends of that platform.
- Pedestrians follow these behavior rules:
    - 1: They walk and behave like real-world humans (avoiding collisions and moving toward their goal).
    - 2: They wait in line if the train is currently unavailable.
        - Two lines beside the gate, the pedestrians choose the closer one
    - 3: When the train arrives, passengers on board get off first before new passengers are allowed to enter.
    - 4: The train has both seats and standing space. passengers prioritize sitting down.
    - 5: Passengers standing in open spaces choose a spot, but they can be pushed if others need to squeeze onto the train.
    - 6: When disembarking, passengers try to avoid others, but they will push through if necessary.
    - 7: If a passenger gets pushed off the train, they wait outside and re-board first once all disembarking passengers have exited.

Here are some detailed requirements:
- Crowd Spawner
    - Configurable spawn interval
    - Configurable global pedestrian limit
    - Configurable goal weights for spawned pedestrians
- Pedestrian
    - Configurable walking speed
